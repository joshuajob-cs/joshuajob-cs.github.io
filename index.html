What Is Elephantelate?

Elephantelate is a one-person web studio. That person is me. I build websites to grow startups.

But Why?

I've always loved building fun projects. When I was 11, I directed super hero movies with my stuffed animals. In 2021, I wrote a lot of poetry. My Freshman year of college I joined the stand up comedy club. I'm a nerd with a flair for the dramatic. And an epic website needs both. As CTO of EduquestGo to Eduquest, I got to build a site for a cause worth shouting about. Now I want to shout about yours. Let's make your debut dramatic!
